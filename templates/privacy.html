Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Minimization Principle

ReTurn adheres to a strict data minimization approach. We collect only the information necessary to provide career re-entry guidance, resume analysis, opportunity matching, and personalized roadmaps. We do not request sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar numbers, bank details, medical records, or biometric data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, and hashed password (passwords are never stored in plain text).
  • Profile Data: Location, education, qualification, skills, career gap details, work preferences, and career goals.
  • Resume Data: Uploaded resume files and extracted text for analysis purposes.
  • Usage Data: Chatbot interactions, roadmap progress, bookmarks, and job applications submitted through the platform.
  • Recruiter Data: Company name, designation, company email, website, and verification details.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate personalized career analysis, skill gap assessments, and learning roadmaps.
  • To analyze uploaded resumes and provide structural and keyword improvement feedback.
  • To match your profile with relevant verified job opportunities.
  • To provide contextual chatbot assistance.
  • To send in-app notifications about relevant opportunities and platform updates.

4. AI Processing

When AI features are enabled, portions of your profile data and resume text may be sent to third-party AI providers (Google Gemini or OpenAI) for processing. This data is used solely for generating personalized guidance and is subject to the respective AI provider's data handling policies.

  • We do not store AI-generated outputs permanently beyond what is saved in your analysis results.
  • AI processing is optional — the platform uses deterministic fallback logic when AI is unavailable.
  • No personal financial or medical data is ever sent to AI services.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored locally in an SQLite database on the server.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard Werkzeug security hashing (pbkdf2:sha256).
  • Session cookies are configured with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax attributes.
  • Uploaded files are stored in a restricted server directory.
  • API keys and secrets are stored exclusively in server-side environment variables, never exposed to the client.

6. Data Sharing

ReTurn does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your profile information is visible only to:

  • You: Full access to all your own data.
  • Verified Recruiters: Only when you explicitly apply to a job listing (application details and linked resume).
  • Platform Administrators: For account management, verification, and moderation purposes.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all associated data (profile, resumes, analyses, applications, bookmarks, chat logs) will be permanently removed from the database.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your stored data through your profile and dashboard.
  • Correction: You can update your profile information at any time.
  • Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: You can download your improved resume and analysis results.

9. Cookies

ReTurn uses essential session cookies for authentication and user state management. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics trackers.

10. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Any material changes will be communicated through in-app notifications. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the platform administrator.
