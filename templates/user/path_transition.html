Career Shift & Industry Transition

Start with the career change you have in mind and why it matters to you. We will check feasibility, suggest practical actions, and identify realistic ways to fund the transition before you work on your resume.

Start with your transition idea

Let’s test the change before rewriting your resume

A clear idea and honest motivation help us recommend a safer, more actionable path.

Feasibility study

  • Interview or survey three people already doing this work.
  • Review ten real job or customer listings and note repeated skills, pay, and entry requirements.
  • Complete one small sample task and ask for feedback before paying for a long course.

Actionable 90-day plan

  1. Days 1–14: Validate demand and list the three skills you must bridge.
  2. Days 15–45: Learn one priority skill and complete a small proof-of-work project.
  3. Days 46–75: Get feedback, improve the project, and practise the target workflow.
  4. Days 76–90: Apply to targeted roles or test the idea with your first pilot client.

Funding and low-risk launch ideas

Learn cheaply: use free public courses, library resources, and employer-sponsored learning before borrowing.
Earn while learning: try a small freelance, volunteer, internship, or project-based assignment to test demand.
Check support: compare scholarships, women-focused schemes, skilling vouchers, and regulated small-business programs.

Transferable Skills Identifier

See how competencies from your origin field map into common digital and operations roles:

Teaching / Academia → Instructional Design & Technical Writing High Overlap

Transferable: Curriculum structuring, breaking down complex topics, audience empathy, document formatting.
Bridge Skills Needed: Markdown, LMS tools, Figma basics, API documentation guidelines.

Customer Support / Admin → Customer Success & Operations High Overlap

Transferable: Escalation handling, cross-team communication, client retention, conflict resolution.
Bridge Skills Needed: CRM software (HubSpot/Salesforce), SQL queries, SLA reporting dashboards.

Accounting / Banking → FinTech Product Operations & Data Analysis Very High Overlap

Transferable: Ledger reconciliation, regulatory compliance, numerical precision, financial auditing.
Bridge Skills Needed: Advanced Python/Pandas, automated ETL scripts, Power BI/Tableau visualization.

Functional Resume Restructuring

When changing careers, a standard chronological resume emphasizes past job titles that do not match the target role. A Hybrid Functional Resume groups your achievements under skill categories (e.g. "Project & Process Management", "Client Communication", "Technical Tools") placed above your chronological employment history.
