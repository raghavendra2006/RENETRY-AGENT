Section A

What is the ReTurn platform?

A dedicated, non-stigmatizing web application designed specifically to address the structural hurdles women encounter when re-entering the workforce after caregiving, health recovery, relocation, or personal breaks.

Who It Is For

Women with Career Pauses

Designed for experienced professionals returning to their field, individuals seeking first-time entry, and those navigating a career pivot into a different industry.

What Problem It Addresses

The 'Gap Penalty' & Information Silos

Traditional hiring systems filter out non-linear resumes. ReTurn replaces automated rejection with structured skill auditing, constructive gap framing, and verified returnships.

What Users Can Do

Actionable Re-Entry Workflow

Audit existing skills, track progress on a 7-stage roadmap, modernize your resume, evaluate startup ideas, and explore verified recruiter & government listings.

Section B

Why career re-entry matters

Career pauses are a standard reality of life. However, returning to work presents practical challenges that require thoughtful preparation rather than marketing platitudes.

Skill Gaps

Tools and frameworks evolve rapidly. Focused refresher coursework brings candidates back to parity with current workplace demands.

Resume Gaps

Unexplained pauses trigger screening drop-offs. Strategic formatting presents gap periods as continuous learning and intentional readiness.

Confidence & Readiness

Extended time away often creates impostor syndrome. Structured milestone roadmaps re-establish professional confidence systematically.

Suitable Opportunities

Identifying employers with explicit returnship programs avoids wasted applications to companies with rigid hiring timelines.

Current Requirements

Understanding hybrid work standards, modern communication tools, and statutory relaxations ensures realistic application targeting.

Section E

Platform capabilities

Only features that are fully implemented and functional in this application:

1. Profile Assessment

Evaluates qualifications, skills, and work preferences to suggest the best re-entry route.

2. 7-Stage Career Roadmap

Track tasks from profile completion through learning, practice, resume, and applications.

3. Resume Analysis (PDF/DOCX)

Extracts actual file text, auditing missing sections, weak action verbs, and role keywords.

Provides concrete STAR rewrites and an editable modernized re-entry resume draft.

Analyzes business ideas, problem clarity, MVP stages, and realistic scheme eligibility.

9. ReTurn Career Assistant

On-demand factual guidance for framing career gaps, navigating roadmap milestones, and government schemes.
